Refund Policy

This policy is designed for subscription checkout review and Paddle-compatible customer support. Last updated: June 5, 2026.

1. Subscription Billing

Paid Alvarix SecureAI plans are billed as monthly subscriptions. When Paddle is enabled, Paddle may act as merchant of record and process checkout, invoices, taxes, and payment disputes.

2. Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation through the account billing flow when available or by contacting support. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically refund the current billing period.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be considered for duplicate charges, accidental purchases, billing errors, inability to access the service caused by Alvarix, or other reasonable customer support cases submitted promptly after billing.

4. Non-Refundable Usage

Fees may be non-refundable when the subscription period has been substantially used, API credits have been consumed, abuse or policy violations occurred, or a custom enterprise agreement states different terms.

5. Billing Disputes

Customers should contact support before initiating a chargeback so the issue can be investigated. Include the account email, invoice ID, charge date, plan, and a clear description of the issue.

6. Paddle Process

Where Paddle processes payments, refunds may be handled through Paddle systems and subject to Paddle review, payment method limitations, tax rules, and processing timelines.

7. Contact

Refund and billing questions may be sent to support@alvarix.ai.
