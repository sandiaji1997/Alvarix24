Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Alvarix SecureAI handles customer account data, API usage data, logs, and security signals. Last updated: June 5, 2026.

1. Data We Collect

We may collect account registration data, contact details, authentication events, API keys metadata, billing status, support requests, and information submitted to API endpoints for risk scoring and fraud detection.

2. API Usage Analytics

We process API usage metrics such as request counts, endpoint usage, response status, quota consumption, timestamps, and account identifiers to operate the service, enforce limits, improve reliability, and support customers.

3. Security Monitoring

We may process IP addresses, request metadata, authentication attempts, API key activity, device or location signals, and error logs to detect abuse, protect accounts, investigate incidents, and maintain platform security.

4. Fraud Detection Processing

Customer-submitted risk signals may be processed to generate risk scores, threat tags, confidence levels, reasons, and decision recommendations. Customers are responsible for ensuring they have a lawful basis to submit data.

5. Customer Account Data

Account data is used for onboarding, authentication, plan management, support, billing readiness, service communications, and compliance administration.

6. Log Retention

Operational logs are retained for a reasonable period needed for security, debugging, analytics, abuse prevention, and legal compliance, unless a separate customer agreement specifies different retention terms.

7. Third-Party Processors

We may use infrastructure, hosting, database, analytics, email, support, and payment processors such as Railway, database providers, and Paddle or other billing processors. These providers process data only as needed to support the service.

8. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, or restriction of personal data. Requests can be submitted through the contact email below.

9. International Operation

Alvarix SecureAI is built for global SaaS operation. Data may be processed in jurisdictions where service providers operate, with reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.
