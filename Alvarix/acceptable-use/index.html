Acceptable Use Policy

This policy explains permitted and prohibited uses of Alvarix SecureAI and the Alvarix Risk API. Last updated: June 5, 2026.

1. Permitted Use

Customers may use Alvarix SecureAI for fraud prevention, transaction risk scoring, account protection, API abuse detection, threat classification, payment intelligence, and related business security workflows.

2. Prohibited Use

Customers may not use the service for illegal activity, malware, credential theft, harassment, unlawful surveillance, discrimination, sanctions evasion, or attempts to attack, overload, probe, or reverse engineer Alvarix systems.

3. Sensitive Decisions

Risk scores and decisions are advisory signals. Customers must apply appropriate human review, compliance controls, and business rules before using Alvarix output in high-impact decisions.

4. Data Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for having appropriate rights and lawful basis to submit event, account, transaction, or payment context to the API.

5. API Protection

Customers must protect API keys, avoid public exposure, respect rate limits, and promptly notify Alvarix if credentials are compromised.

6. Enforcement

Alvarix may suspend or limit access for abuse, platform risk, illegal activity, non-payment, security incidents, or violations of this policy.
